## Getting there

The following services stop at **Porte de la Chapelle**, near the venue:

- **Métro:** line 12.
- **Tram:** line T3b.
- **Bus:** line 302.

See the [university's venue information](https://www.pantheonsorbonne.fr/universite/projets/centre-chapelle) for access details.

## Accessibility

The university describes the building as fully accessible, with adapted circulation routes, teaching rooms, amphitheatres and toilets, including spaces for wheelchair users. If you have specific access requirements for the workshop, please contact {{< contact >}}.

## Format

A full day, in person. Talks will be recorded and made available after the workshop.

See the [programme]({{< relref "programme" >}}) for the day's schedule.
